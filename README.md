# Stormwater Intelligence Platform — Original Prototype

This repository contains an early public prototype of the Stormwater Intelligence Platform.

The prototype explored how interactive 3D mapping, weather information, federal environmental datasets, and AI-assisted interfaces could be brought together in one environmental intelligence system.

## Prototype Features

* CesiumJS 3D Earth visualization
* Weather and precipitation layers
* Federal environmental-data connections
* Watershed and flood-context visualization
* Natural-language environmental queries
* Storm and hazard visualization experiments
* Interactive mapping controls

## Project Status

This repository represents an earlier development stage and is preserved as part of the Stormwater Intelligence development history.

It is not the current production codebase, and some demonstrations, integrations, model references, links, and architectural descriptions from the original prototype may no longer represent the active Stormwater Intelligence system.

Current development focuses on practical environmental services and traceable workflow outputs supporting:

* California stormwater compliance
* SWPPP and Construction General Permit work
* Watershed and receiving-water research
* TMDL and impaired-water analysis
* Precipitation and weather readiness
* Sediment-risk calculations
* Environmental mapping and GIS
* Water-treatment and field-support operations
* Structured workbooks, reports, and supporting evidence

## Stormwater Intelligence LLC

Stormwater Intelligence LLC provides stormwater compliance, water-treatment, environmental field-support, watershed-analysis, mapping, and documentation services for contractors, developers, engineering firms, industrial facilities, remediation projects, and public agencies.

The Stormwater Intelligence Platform supports that work by combining geospatial visualization, authoritative public environmental data, weather intelligence, and AI-assisted research.
